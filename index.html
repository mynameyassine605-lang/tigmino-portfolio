The Designer

Started in finance.
Ended up here.

My journey began in Finance & Banking, but I quickly realized my passion belonged to design rather than numbers. That decision led me to specialize in multimedia design, where I discovered branding, motion, and 3D visualization. Over the years, Tigmino Creates has evolved into my creative studio, helping businesses build memorable visual identities, engaging digital experiences, and high-quality visual content across multiple industries.

Today, I work as Lead Graphic & Motion Designer at OG Communication while collaborating with international clients through Tigmino Creates. From brand identities and marketing campaigns to cinematic 3D visuals and motion design, every project is approached with the same attention to detail, strategy, and craftsmanship. I believe great design comes from thoughtful execution, not shortcuts.

I work directly with every client from the first conversation to the final delivery, ensuring clear communication, transparency, and a collaborative creative process. Native in Arabic, fluent in English, and professionally comfortable in French, I enjoy working with clients from different industries and cultural backgrounds.

How I Work

How a project actually runs.

01

Discover

Before anything gets made, I need to understand what actually needs solving. That means asking questions most designers skip — about the audience, the competition, and what 'success' actually looks like for this specific project.

02

Research

References and visual audits. I map the landscape so we know exactly what exists before we decide where to go. Directions should be chosen, not stumbled into.

03

Strategy

The brief for the visual work. Tone, hierarchy, the story we're telling and the one we're deliberately not telling. This is the step that makes the design make sense later.

04

Design

Concepts, iterations, and the kind of honest refinement that happens when you're willing to throw away a good idea because a better one showed up. This is where most of the time goes.

05

Refinement

Feedback taken seriously, not just acknowledged. Every revision round has a reason. Nothing gets marked done until it's actually done.

06

Delivery

Organized files, correct formats, and clear documentation. You leave with everything needed to use the work without having to come back and ask.
